Martin Garrix went on his largest career tour in 2026, which he deemed his 'Americas Tour'. I was lucky enough to have the opporotunity to create concert visuals for one of the songs performed in said tour: Poison.
On top of the visuals I made myself, I was also a production lead for a larger group of designers on the show that ended up making visuals for several other songs across the tour.
