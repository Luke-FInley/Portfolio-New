Recently I've developed a campaign setting for Dungeons and Dragons 5th edition called "The Gambler's Guide to Valorus". I've constructed this world from scratch with loose inspiration from early 20th century New York crime families in combination with a high magic, utopian-presenting society. I've built this setting over several campaigns with contributions to the world from my players over the years. I finally decided to put all of this into writing, get art, and make it "real" only recently and to-date it's one of my proudest projects. https://anyflip.com/zbtza/hrda
