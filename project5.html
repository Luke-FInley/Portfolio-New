Running Late was a project for a class I took in 2025 that utilized Houdini and Adobe After Effects. The project originally flew under my radar until only a couple days before the due date. I also decided to spontaniously use a program I had never touched before, thus I learned what I could of Houdini in two days, and applied the knowledge I had from After Effects to make this panicked short submission.
And while this project is not exactly one of my favorites, I'm ultimately proud of the result and the time management skills it fostered.
